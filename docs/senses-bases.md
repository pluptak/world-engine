# Basis codes

Every `perceive` answer carries a `basis_code`: why it is true, false or unknown. The table it
applies is in [senses.md](senses.md).

The false bases are `too_far`, `no_such_entity`, `no_such_event`, `no_target`, `observer_destroyed`,
`unpowered`, `no_sense_capacity`, `authored`, `odourless`, `quiet`, `not_touching`, `abstract`,
`concealed`, `enclosed`, `not_perceptible` and `location_unlit`; the unknown ones are
`uncovered_sense` and `engine_incapable`; the true ones are `same_location`, `same_location_lit`,
`adjacent_open_door_lit`, `adjacent_loud_event`, `own_body` and `camera` ([camera.md](camera.md)),
and `intercom` ([intercom.md](intercom.md)).

A projection ([projection.md](projection.md)) names an event's entity only to an observer who sensed
it by something besides hearing; one who only heard it is told `here` (`same_location`) or
`next_door` (`adjacent_loud_event`) in place of the entity.
